The History

Four thousand years,
one leaf.

2737 BCE — Chapter I

The emperor
and the leaf

افسانوی آغاز

Legend tells of Shen Nong, the divine farmer of ancient China, who rested beneath a wild tree as a few leaves drifted into his boiling water.

618–907 CE — Chapter II

Poetry
in the cup

کپ میں شاعری

An atmospheric chamber where Tang-dynasty masters meditated beside glowing clay stoves, and tea became ceremony.

8th–14th Century — Chapter III

The Silk Road
caravans

شاہراہِ ریشم کے قافلے

Precious compressed blocks of dark tea, traded hand to hand across desert oasis hubs from Xi'an to Samarkand.

16th–18th Century — Chapter IV

Courts of
spice and milk

مغل شاہی محل

Ornate brass kettles and aromatic whole spices inside royal Mughal courtyards — the birth of chai as we know it.

Present Day — Chapter V

The modern
street corner

چائے کا ٹھیلا

Steaming doodh patti, poured from a height, amid the noise and color of every city street it's ever met.
